Directions to Boelter 6426

Street Directions:

  • From any direction on Interstate 405, look for sign that says Westwood/UCLA.
  • Exit Wilshire Blvd. “East” and continue east on Wilshire.
  • While on Wilshire Blvd., move into your left lane because you will be making a left turn three blocks ahead in heavy traffic.
  • Continue to go straight ahead (approximately 5 lights or blocks) to the kiosk in the middle of the street.
  • Tell them your “confirmation number” or name. You will be given your parking pass and directions to Lot 9.
Walking Directions:
  • From Lot 9, exit level 3, cross the ally (northward) to the Engineering IV building.
  • Upon entering the building turn to the right and take one of the elevators to the 5th floor.
  • Exit elevator and turn right. Walk across the bridge into Boelter Hall. You will notice, this is the 6th floor in Boelter Hall.
  • Walk to the end of the hall and turn left. Go to Room 6426 and ask for Professor Wesel's office.
