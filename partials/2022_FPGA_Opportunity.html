2022 FPGA Internship Opportunity

Communication Systems Laboratory

Undergraduate Research Opportunities

FPGA Implementation of LDPC Encoder/Decoder

Starting October 2022

We are excited to announce an opportunity for two UCLA undergraduates to participate in paid research in Professor Wesel’s Communications Systems Laboratory starting in October of 2022.

Working as a part of a team, two UCLA undergraduates will assist in implementing an Field Programmable Gate Array (FPGA) Encoder/Decoder pair for a Low-Density Parity-Check (LDPC) code and proving simulation results verifying its performance .

FPGA experience is required. The preferred skill set includes the ability to work well on a team as well as proficiencies in Verilog, C++ (CS31), Matlab, probability (ECE 131A), and communications systems (ECE 132A).

More information about the CSL can be found at http://www.seas.ucla.edu/csl.

Applications should send a resume, cover letter, and unofficial transcript to Professor Wesel at wesel@ucla.edu with the subject line “Applicant for FPGA LDPC Implementation.” Applicants should be available to start immediately for fall quarter. The position can continue into winter, spring, and summer based on performance. experience. Review of applications will begin Tuesday, October 11, at 5 p.m. and continue until the positions are filled.
